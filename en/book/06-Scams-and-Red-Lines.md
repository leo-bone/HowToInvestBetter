# VI. Scams and Red Lines

Listen to what regulators say plainly, and you can skip decades of tuition. Former CBIRC chairman Guo Shuqing put it bluntly at the 2018 Lujiazui Forum: "Yields above 6% should raise a question, above 8% are very dangerous, above 10% you should prepare to lose all principal." This isn't a scare—it's the regulator's public characterization of illegal fund-raising schemes. The same logic is written into the system: the 2018 *Guiding Opinions on Regulating Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions* (Asset Management Rules, PBOC [2018] No. 106), Articles 2 and 6, state that "financial institutions may not promise guaranteed principal and returns when conducting asset management business," thoroughly breaking rigid redemption; the State Council's 2021 *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737), Article 25, states coldly that "losses from participating in illegal fund raising shall be borne by the participants themselves"—meaning if you step into illegal absorption, the state won't advance or buy back your lost money.

At its core every entry in this chapter says one thing: anything "high return, low risk, and steady," anything "act now, keep it secret, don't tell anyone," is almost certainly a scam. Every item below is a danger signal explicitly warned of in official documents—not my made-up "experience."

How to use this chapter: treat it as your "negative list." For any investment opportunity, match it against the 18 entries below one by one; if it hits even one, no matter how impressive the other side's background or how tempting the return, default to "it's a scam"—retreat first, verify later. Miss rather than step on a mine—because in a scam you lose not just the return, but the principal itself.

This chapter's entries are grouped by theme below (entry numbers in parentheses):

- I. The lie of guaranteed principal and returns (regulatory red line): Entry 1 guaranteed return / sure win, Entry 4 high-yield wealth "guaranteed high return," Entry 10 managed-account promising a floor
- II. Illegal stock-tipping and call-out: Entry 2 tip groups / teacher-led trades / insider news, Entry 14 WeChat/QQ tip groups with tiered harvesting
- III. Ponzi and money games: Entry 3 Ponzi / money game, Entry 16 money game / rebate game, Entry 11 illegal fund raising and pyramid recruitment
- IV. Crypto and fake-concept speculation: Entry 5 crypto speculation, Entry 12 fake "blockchain / metaverse / AI" concept speculation
- V. Fake platforms and bucket shops: Entry 9 fake platforms / black apps, Entry 13 virtual / bucket-shop platforms, Entry 15 crude-oil / precious-metals / stamp-coin spot black platforms
- VI. Off-exchange margin and original shares: Entry 6 off-exchange margin / illegal trading platforms, Entry 7 "original shares / unlisted equity" scams
- VII. Pig-butchering and identity impersonation: Entry 8 pig-butchering (raise then slaughter), Entry 17 impersonating officials / AI face-swap customer-service scams
- VIII. Elderly scams: Entry 18 elderly / wellness wealth scams

### 1. "Guaranteed return / sure win"—run on sight
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=no payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: The "perfect opportunity" you might miss (actually a trap).
- In plain terms: In finance there's no "high return AND no risk" free lunch. Whoever dares write "guaranteed," "sure win," "zero risk" into a contract is guaranteeing they'll take your principal. Guo Shuqing's 2018 Lujiazui Forum words characterize exactly this kind of talk.
- Payoff: Keep all your principal and family savings. The regulatory red line is clear: the Asset Management Rules (PBOC [2018] No. 106), Articles 2 and 6, forbid any asset-management product from "promising guaranteed principal and returns," and Article 19 lists the criteria for identifying rigid redemption and sets penalties; Guo Shuqing's 2018 line—"yield above 6% raises a question, above 8% is dangerous, above 10% prepare to lose all principal." Once a product is deemed illegal fund raising, per Article 25 of the *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising*, "losses from participating in illegal fund raising shall be borne by the participants themselves"—the state advances and buys back nothing. Avoiding it swaps the "promised high interest" for principal safely in hand.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Guo Shuqing (2018). Speech at the 10th Lujiazui Forum (People's Daily Online report). <https://money.people.com.cn/n1/2018/0614/c42877-30059109.html>; People's Bank of China and four departments (2018). *Guiding Opinions on Regulating Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions* (PBOC [2018] No. 106, Articles 2, 6, 19). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2018-05/04/content_5287860.htm>; State Council (2021). *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737, Article 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>
- Note: The 6%/8%/10% line is the regulator's experiential warning against "high-yield talk," not a legal threshold; specific cases are judged by whether they constitute illegal fund raising and whether they promise guaranteed principal and returns.

### 2. Tip groups, teacher-led trades, insider news
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=no payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: The lure of "copying the big shots' homework."
- In plain terms: A method that really makes steady money won't be sold to you for 9.9 yuan in a WeChat group. Call-out is essentially "find enough people that half are right by chance—collect on the wins, vanish on the losses"; profit screenshots are almost forgeable.
- Payoff: Avoid illegal stock-tipping and pig-butchering, keep your principal. Securities investment advisory is a franchised business—the CSRC states no institution or individual may engage in it without CSRC approval (*Securities Law*, *Interim Measures for the Administration of Securities and Futures Investment Advisory*). Illegal tip-giving's three hallmarks are "no business qualification, recommending stocks, charging service fees." The U.S. SEC likewise warns: "Guaranteed high investment returns are the hallmark of a Ponzi scheme"—any "sure win" is a red flag. In 2025 the Shenzhen CSRC disclosed several routines—"buy expensive goods, get free tips," "illegal tip-giving under the guise of training," "AI stock-picking scams"—where victims often win small first then lose big, with little recourse. Don't join groups, don't add teachers; that alone dodges the most common kind of sheep-shearing.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: China Securities Regulatory Commission (no date). Risk alert on illegal securities investment advisory. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/sichuan/c104903/c3243464/content.shtml>; Shenzhen CSRC (2025). Risk alert on guarding against illegal stock-tipping scams. <https://jr.sz.gov.cn/sjrb/xxgk/tzzjy/content/post_12213565.html>; SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Ponzi Schemes Targeting Seniors. <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_ponziseniors>
- Note: Licensed advisers can be verified on the CSRC / Securities Association of China website; anything asking for transfers to personal accounts, buying goods to join groups, or sending links privately to download apps is treated as illegal.

### 3. Ponzi / money game: paying old investors with new money
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=no payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A bit of "get in early and snowball" greed.
- In plain terms: Recognize three features—① promising unreasonable fixed high interest; ② growing a downline by recruiting people; ③ unclear where the money goes. Early or late, you lose; the only difference is who catches the last baton.
- Payoff: Avoid a scheme that will inevitably collapse and most likely wipe you out. The U.S. SEC defines a Ponzi scheme as "paying old investors with money from new investors," with red flags including "high return, zero risk promises, unlicensed sellers, abnormally stable long-run returns." History's biggest Ponzi—the Madoff case—had about 65 billion USD of fictitiously inflated account value (pleaded guilty in 2009, sentenced to 150 years); after the collapse retail principal was nearly zero. Domestically, per Article 25 of the *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising*, participation losses are self-borne. Holding the line "don't invest in cash flows you can't understand" dodges the systematic zeroing risk.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Ponzi Schemes Targeting Seniors. <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_ponziseniors>; State Council (2021). *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737, Articles 2, 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>; Ministry of Public Security (2024). 2023 telecom-fraud case data bulletin (reported by China Worker Net). <https://www.workercn.cn/c/2024-01-10/8108310.shtml>
- Note: Madoff's "65 billion USD" refers to the fictitiously inflated book value of his accounts, not actual defrauded principal; it illustrates the sheer scale of a Ponzi's "borrow-new-to-pay-old."

### 4. High-yield wealth "guaranteed high return": the contradiction is the answer
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: The thought of earning a few extra points.
- In plain terms: The treasury yield is the risk-free yardstick. Any product that dares to out-yield it by a wide margin while claiming no risk is hiding that extra spread as risk premium—eventually repaid with principal.
- Payoff: Avoid default and blow-up, keep your principal. The Asset Management Rules (PBOC [2018] No. 106), Article 2, state "financial institutions may not promise guaranteed principal and returns when conducting asset management business," and "may not advance funds to redeem in any form" when repayment is difficult; Article 19 deems "guaranteed principal and returns violating net-value principles, rolling issuance to guarantee principal and returns, self-financed repayment" all rigid redemption, with penalties. Guo Shuqing's 2018 scale is 6%/8%/10%—above 10% prepare to lose all principal. The risk-free rate is only so high; the extra "return" is hidden credit risk—avoiding it means not paying for someone else's bad debt.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: People's Bank of China and four departments (2018). *Guiding Opinions on Regulating Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions* (PBOC [2018] No. 106, Articles 2, 19). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2018-05/04/content_5287860.htm>; Guo Shuqing (2018). Speech at the 10th Lujiazui Forum (People's Daily Online report). <https://money.people.com.cn/n1/2018/0614/c42877-30059109.html>; State Council (2021). *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737, Article 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>
- Note: The Asset Management Rules apply to asset-management products of licensed financial institutions; many "guaranteed high-return" wealth products are issued by unlicensed entities and fall directly into illegal fund raising.

### 5. Crypto speculation: treat it as a casino, not an asset
<!-- tags: money=little time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: Principal possibly invested (high volatility, easy to zero, platform run, not legally protected domestically).
- In plain terms: Bitcoin generates no cash flow, pays no dividend, and swings wildly—essentially a speculative item. A small position as a lottery is OK; loading up as an investment is betting your life, and domestic trading and speculation are explicitly not legally protected.
- Payoff: Avoid domestically unprotected speculation and platform run risk, keep your principal. The regulatory characterization is extremely clear: the PBOC and six other departments' Sept 4, 2017 notice characterized token issuance financing (ICO) as "unapproved illegal public financing" and ordered all ICOs to stop immediately; the PBOC and nine other departments' Sept 24, 2021 *Notice on Further Preventing and Dealing with the Risks of Virtual-Currency Trading and Speculation* (PBOC [2021] No. 237) states that "virtual-currency-related business activities are illegal financial activities," overseas exchanges serving domestic residents are equally illegal, and participating violates public order—"relevant civil legal acts are void, losses self-borne." The SEC also warns crypto scams often promise "20%–50% risk-free returns" then abscond with funds. For most people it returns no steady income, only thrills and zeroing.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: People's Bank of China and seven departments (2017). *Announcement on Preventing the Risks of Token Issuance Financing*. <https://money.people.com.cn/bank/n1/2017/0904/c202331-29514147.html>; People's Bank of China and ten departments (2021). *Notice on Further Preventing and Dealing with the Risks of Virtual-Currency Trading and Speculation* (PBOC [2021] No. 237). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-09/25/content_5639206.htm>; SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Watch Out for Fraudulent Digital Asset and "Crypto" Trading Websites. <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_fraudulentdigitalasset>
- Note: Licensed domestic financial institutions and payment institutions may not provide accounts, clearing, etc. for virtual-currency business; so-called "overseas compliant exchanges" remain illegal financial activities for domestic residents.

### 6. Off-exchange margin, illegal trading platforms
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A fantasy of leveraging up principal.
- In plain terms: Regulated leverage has strict rules and liquidation lines. Off-exchange margin's rules are set by the other side—drop a bit and they move the line to swallow your principal; it's a gun pointed at you.
- Payoff: Avoid the systematic traps of misappropriated funds, platform shutdown, and opaque forced-liquidation rules; keep your principal. The CSRC and Ministry of Public Security jointly characterize: under the *Criminal Law*, *Securities Law*, and *Administrative Regulations on Futures Trading*, off-exchange margin operation is illegal securities/futures activity, and if it constitutes a crime it is prosecuted as illegal business operation or fraud. In 2020 the CSRC transferred and reported 89 off-exchange margin leads to public security, coordinated on 19 major cases, and arrested over 700 people; the Chongqing "Cuohe Wang" case offered 8x leverage, involved 55 billion yuan of trading, and 153 were arrested. Participating in off-exchange margin means "your interests are unprotected and you may suffer large property loss."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: CSRC and Ministry of Public Security (2021). 2020 typical cases of off-exchange margin crime. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/jiangxi/c100771/c1764378/content.shtml>; CSRC (2021). Introduction to the Chongqing "Cuohe Wang" stock-margin case. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/shaanxi/c105610/cbde7843908ca42e5a2be4b4eb50aeb94/content.shtml>; State Council (2021). *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737, Article 19). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>
- Note: Any margin using a "sub-account split system," advertising high leverage, or asking you to download a non-official app is mostly illegal or a "virtual platform"—open an account with a regulated broker instead.

### 7. "Original shares / unlisted equity" scams
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A bit of "get rich on IPO" imagination.
- In plain terms: Real quality unlisted equity never reaches a stranger. The "original shares" they sell you are mostly unsellable, or outright fake. Non-standard + high threshold + pressuring you to decide = a scam, all three at once.
- Payoff: Avoid non-standard investments with zero liquidity, a black-box of information, and easy total loss; keep your principal. Article 19 of the *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* explicitly lists "absorbing funds under the name of issuing or transferring equity, claims, or raising funds" as suspected illegal fund raising; Article 25 states participation losses are self-borne. Unlisted equity is a typical non-standard asset—no public trading venue, no fair valuation—and scammers press with "about to list" or "internal channel." The SEC also notes private placements are only non-public offerings to qualified investors; peddling "original shares" to the public is a violation. Not touching "equity" you can't understand or find filed keeps your money safe.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2021). *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737, Articles 19, 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>; SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Ponzi Schemes Targeting Seniors (private-placement red flags). <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_ponziseniors>; China Securities Regulatory Commission (no date). Risk alert on illegal securities investment advisory. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/sichuan/c104903/c3243464/content.shtml>
- Note: Domestic legal equity financing should be registered/filed with the CSRC and conducted on compliant venues; "original shares" peddled on streets or in WeChat groups are almost always illegal securities activity or fraud.

### 8. Pig-butchering: raise then slaughter
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: Trust and time (set up by "romance / friendship").
- In plain terms: The nicer they are to you and the more they push "let's make money together," the colder you should be. Real affection won't lead you into an investment platform; any "platform deposit, can't withdraw" pattern is pure pig-butchering.
- Payoff: Avoid being lured to deposit on a fake platform and ultimately unable to withdraw; keep your principal. Ministry of Public Security data is stark: in 2023 national public security organs cracked 437,000 telecom-fraud cases, the National Anti-Fraud Center issued 9.4 million fund-warning directives and 13.89 million in-person warnings, and urgently intercepted 328.8 billion yuan of involved funds. The U.S. FTC warns: an online-romance "teaching you to invest" is 100% pig-butchering—in 2021 Americans lost 547 million USD to romance scams, median loss 2,400 USD; the SEC also notes crypto "pig-butchering" often promises 20%–50% risk-free returns then absconds with funds. Going cold, not depositing, means not being "fattened then slaughtered."
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Ministry of Public Security (2024). 2023 results bulletin on combating telecom-fraud (reported by China Worker Net). <https://www.workercn.cn/c/2024-01-10/8108310.shtml>; FTC (2022). FTC Data Show Romance Scams Hit Record High; $547 Million Reported Lost in 2021. <https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/press-releases/2022/02/ftc-data-show-romance-scams-hit-record-high-547-million-reported>; SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Watch Out for Fraudulent Digital Asset and "Crypto" Trading Websites. <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_fraudulentdigitalasset>
- Note: Pig-butchering often lets you withdraw small amounts first to "build trust," then lures large deposits; once they demand "pay tax / unfreeze fee to withdraw," it's definitely fraud—call the police and the anti-fraud center immediately.

### 9. Fake platforms / black apps
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A bit of luck on "high-yield platforms."
- In plain terms: An app from an unknown source, promising high yields, with customer service pushing you to deposit more—assume it's fake first. Regulated brokers/funds are all in official app stores and licensed directories.
- Payoff: Avoid the scam of "locked on deposit, can't withdraw," keep your principal. The CSRC warns: fake platforms closely mimic real trading platforms in name, icon, and interface, but are self-developed "virtual platforms"—the core logic is "lure deposits early → funds go straight to the scammer's personal account → delay with frozen-account/fee excuses and eventually run." In 2023 the Ministry of Public Security handled 8.364 million fraud-related domains and intercepted 2.75 billion fraud calls, showing the scale of black platforms. Article 25 of the *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* states participation losses are self-borne. Downloading trading software only from official app stores and licensed institutions' sites cuts off 90% of black-app entry points.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: China Securities Regulatory Commission (no date). Risk alert on preventing illegal securities/futures activities (online fraud with forged trading platforms). <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/xinjiang/c105515/c7613926/content.shtml>; Ministry of Public Security (2024). 2023 results bulletin on combating telecom-fraud (reported by China Worker Net). <https://www.workercn.cn/c/2024-01-10/8108310.shtml>; State Council (2021). *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737, Article 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>
- Note: To tell real from fake: check the CSRC / Securities Association of China licensed-institution directory, verify the official domain, refuse transfers to personal accounts; don't download look-alike platforms with similar names or icons.

### 10. Managed-account promising a floor
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A bit of "leave it to an expert" peace of mind.
- In plain terms: A trader who dares sign "guaranteed floor" is either breaking the law or preparing to run. Real pros don't need your money; handing your account to an unqualified individual means you mostly eat the loss yourself.
- Payoff: Avoid the risk that "floor agreements" are mostly void in law and hard to recover from when you lose; keep your principal. The Asset Management Rules (PBOC [2018] No. 106), Article 2, forbid financial institutions from "promising guaranteed principal and returns," and Article 19 deems floor arrangements rigid redemption with penalties; non-financial institutions illegally promising guaranteed principal and returns are "punished more severely per law" (Article 30). The Securities Law requires securities investment advisory to be licensed; unqualified managed trading is itself illegal, and so-called "floor clauses" are mostly ruled void by courts for violating mandatory provisions, with low recovery. Guo Shuqing also said in 2018: "Guaranteed principal can't have high return—that's fraud." Not handing your account to a "floor teacher" means not handing your principal to legal risk.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: People's Bank of China and four departments (2018). *Guiding Opinions on Regulating Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions* (PBOC [2018] No. 106, Articles 2, 19, 30). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2018-05/04/content_5287860.htm>; Guo Shuqing (2018). Speech at the 10th Lujiazui Forum (People's Daily Online report). <https://money.people.com.cn/n1/2018/0614/c42877-30059109.html>; China Securities Regulatory Commission (no date). Risk alert on illegal securities investment advisory. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/sichuan/c104903/c3243464/content.shtml>
- Note: In judicial practice "floor clauses" are often ruled void for violating fairness and financial regulation; even if valid, unqualified individuals often have no property to enforce against, so recovery is low.

### 11. Illegal fund raising and pyramid recruitment
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A bit of the "recruit a downline, earn commission" lure.
- In plain terms: Anything "you only earn by bringing people in, tiered rebates" — treat it as a pyramid first. It relies on connections, not asset growth; collapse is only a matter of time.
- Payoff: Avoid the "pyramid" structure—the bottom always takes the bag, the organizers are criminally liable; keep your principal and social credit. Article 2 of the *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* defines illegal fund raising as "absorbing funds from unspecified objects by promising principal and interest repayment without permission," and Article 19 lists "issuing or transferring equity/claims, asset management, virtual currency" as fund-absorption scenarios; Article 25 states participation losses are self-borne, state doesn't advance. In 2023 the Ministry of Public Security cracked 437,000 telecom-fraud and other cases and intercepted 328.8 billion yuan, much of it pyramid rebate games. The SEC notes Ponzi/pyramid schemes run on "paying old investors with new money" and eventually collapse. Not recruiting, not investing in "tiered rebates," means not being the denominator for a scam.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2021). *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* (State Council Order No. 737, Articles 2, 19, 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm>; Ministry of Public Security (2024). 2023 results bulletin on combating telecom-fraud (reported by China Worker Net). <https://www.workercn.cn/c/2024-01-10/8108310.shtml>; SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Ponzi Schemes Targeting Seniors. <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_ponziseniors>
- Note: Pyramid illegal fund raising often lures in with "static returns" first, then expands with "dynamic recruitment bonuses"; you can be both victim and accomplice if you recruit a downline—stay away and report it.

### 12. Fake "blockchain / metaverse / AI" concept speculation
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=some payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A bit of FOMO on the hot trend.
- In plain terms: Every trend sprouts a pile of "anything-tagged-jumps" shells. A real tech company doesn't pull you into a deposit in a group chat; if you can't see how it makes money, it's betting on a label.
- Payoff: Avoid speculative plays packaged in new concepts with no real cash flow; keep your principal. Regulators have long watched this sheep-shearing via trends: the PBOC and seven departments' Sept 4, 2017 notice characterized token financing as illegal public financing; the ten departments' Sept 24, 2021 notice (PBOC [2021] No. 237) states all virtual-currency-related business is illegal financial activity, and overseas exchanges serving domestic residents are equally illegal. The CSRC has repeatedly warned against illegal tip-giving and non-standard-asset speculation using "blockchain, metaverse, AI" concepts. The SEC also notes crypto scams disguise themselves with "complex new-tech talk + high risk-free returns." Article 25 of the Regulations makes participation losses self-borne. Not investing in projects that "only talk concepts, not cash flow" means not catching the last baton of the concept bubble.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: People's Bank of China and ten departments (2021). *Notice on Further Preventing and Dealing with the Risks of Virtual-Currency Trading and Speculation* (PBOC [2021] No. 237). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-09/25/content_5639206.htm>; People's Bank of China and seven departments (2017). *Announcement on Preventing the Risks of Token Issuance Financing*. <https://money.people.com.cn/bank/n1/2017/0904/c202331-29514147.html>; SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Watch Out for Fraudulent Digital Asset and "Crypto" Trading Websites. <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_fraudulentdigitalasset>
- Note: Real blockchain/AI companies raise via proper equity or public markets; anything raising via "concept coins," "internal shares," or "private-domain deposits" is interwoven with illegal fund raising and fraud.

### 13. Virtual platform / bucket shop: you think you're trading, but you're betting against the house
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=no payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: A few minutes to check whether the platform connects to a real exchange.
- In plain terms: On a real platform your order goes to the exchange; a fake platform is just an app showing numbers, with money straight into the scammer's pocket. Anything asking you to download a non-official app, watching withdrawal speed instead of quotes, assume it's a virtual platform.
- Payoff: Avoid the fake platform of "can't withdraw when you win, all yours when you lose," keep your principal. The CSRC clearly warns: a forged trading platform is a "virtual platform" tool—funds never enter the real market but go straight to the scammer-controlled account, eventually delayed with "freeze / fees" until the platform disappears. In 2023 the Ministry of Public Security handled 8.364 million fraud-related domains and intercepted 328.8 billion yuan, much of it virtual platforms. The SEC notes such platforms often promise 20%–50% risk-free returns then cut contact. Article 25 of the Regulations makes participation losses self-borne. Checking "does it connect to a real exchange, is it from an official app store" exposes the bucket shop's fundamental scam.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: China Securities Regulatory Commission (no date). Risk alert on preventing illegal securities/futures activities (online fraud with forged trading platforms). <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/xinjiang/c105515/c7613926/content.shtml>; Ministry of Public Security (2024). 2023 results bulletin on combating telecom-fraud (reported by China Worker Net). <https://www.workercn.cn/c/2024-01-10/8108310.shtml>; SEC (no date). Investor Alert: Watch Out for Fraudulent Digital Asset and "Crypto" Trading Websites. <https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/ia_fraudulentdigitalasset>
- Note: A virtual platform's backend can change prices at will and fabricate "profits" to lure you to add; any "can't withdraw profits, must pay margin/fee to withdraw" is a fraud signal.

### 14. WeChat/QQ tip groups with tiered harvesting
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=no payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: The lure of "free hot stocks" after joining the group.
- In plain terms: Free entry, daily profit screenshots—not to teach you to make money, but to finally make you the bag-holder or pay membership. If they had a steady winning method, why bring you along?
- Payoff: Avoid the standard script of "sweetener first → split on calls → reverse-shear the sheep," keep your principal. The CSRC defines illegal tip-giving's three elements: "no business qualification, recommending stocks, charging service fees"; the Shenzhen CSRC's 2025 disclosures of "buy expensive goods, get free tips," "tip-giving under education training," "big-data/AI stock-picking tips" show the group's "teacher" and "profitable members" are mostly shills, and followers often lose big with little recourse. The SEC also lists "unlicensed seller + sure-win promise" as a red flag. Not joining private groups, not buying "membership/VIP hot stocks," cuts off the tiered-harvesting entry.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: China Securities Regulatory Commission (no date). ### 16. Money game / rebate game: paying interest from recruiting
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=no payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: Vigilance against "static returns + dynamic recruitment bonuses."
- In plain terms: "Deposit earns daily interest, recruit a downline for another layer" — that's a Ponzi's ID card. Whenever returns come from new money, not real profit, running is only a matter of time.
- Payoff: Avoid the Ponzi structure, keep your principal and relationships. The regulatory red line is clear: Article 2 of the *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* defines "promising principal and interest repayment to absorb funds from unspecified objects" as illegal fund raising, Article 19 lists equity/claims/asset-management/virtual-currency fund absorption, and Article 25 states "losses from participating in illegal fund raising shall be borne by the participants themselves." The SEC defines a Ponzi as "paying old investors with money from new investors," with red flags including "high return, zero risk, abnormally stable long-run returns." In 2023 the Ministry of Public Security cracked 437,000 telecom-fraud and other cases and intercepted 328.8 billion yuan. Not investing in "recruit-rebate," not building a downline, means not becoming a scam's funding source and victim.
- Evidence grade: A

### 18. Elderly / wellness wealth scams
<!-- tags: money=0 time=little will=no payoff=high scope=money impact=self+family -->
- Cost: The warm offensive of "high-yield elderly apartment / elderly bed / wellness wealth."
- In plain terms: Show this to your parents. Anything "deposit for elderly care / bed, promising high-yield buyback" — treat as a scam first. A real elderly-care product won't make you mortgage the house deed.
- Payoff: Avoid "house-for-pension" / "prepaid elderly care" illegal fund raising targeting the elderly; keep retirement money and property. The Supreme People's Court on Aug 24, 2022 published 6 typical elderly-fraud cases, exposing six routines: pre-depositing high interest for "elderly care services," post-sale rebates for "elderly-care projects / apartments," inflated pricing for "elderly-care products / collectibles," luring to mortgage the house to buy wealth via "house-for-pension," fraudulently charging for "pension insurance" agency, and "elderly-care help" free clinics selling overpriced health products; by July 2022 the nationwide courts had accepted 1,394 elderly-fraud cases and executed over 2 billion yuan of property under the special campaign. Article 25 of the *Regulations on Preventing and Dealing with Illegal Fund Raising* makes participation losses self-borne. Think more, consult children more, and distrust "high rebates"—that guards your parents' "retirement money."
