📖 30-Second Guide: How to use this handbook

Every recommendation uses the same ledger. You only answer four questions:

  1. What does it cost? Money, time, willpower, or permanent loss of principal?
  2. What does it return? Long-run real return, lower volatility, tax savings, or avoiding going to zero?
  3. How strong is the evidence? [A] authoritative stats / top journals / official docs ｜ [B] single studies / institutional reports ｜ [C] reasonable inference / regulations
  4. Where is it from? Only journals and official documents—no self-media or marketing accounts.
